# Using videotapes.io data and images

videotapes.io is a non-commercial, community archive of VHS sleeve artwork. We want the catalogue to be open and
useful, and the scans to stay fair to the people and companies who made them. In short: **take the data freely; use
the images by linking to them, not by copying them in bulk.**

## The catalogue data: free to use

- The metadata (which film, region, country, label, catalogue number, year, certificate, release and case type, sizes,
  folds, fingerprints) is **[CC0 1.0](LICENSE)**: no rights reserved. Copy it, mirror it, back it up, build on it,
  commercially or not.
- Credit is appreciated, not required: "Data from [videotapes.io](https://videotapes.io)".
- Film titles and years come from [TMDb](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDb API but is not
  endorsed or certified by TMDb. Plots, cast, genres and posters are TMDb's: look them up there by `tmdbId`.
- Data releases cover real releases only. Fan-made covers stay on the site and in the API (when asked for), not in the
  downloads.

## The images: link, don't bulk-copy

The scans are **© their respective rights holders** (studios, distributors, artists). videotapes.io shows them for
preservation and research. The CC0 licence covers the data, not the images.

- **Do** show covers in your own app, library or research by linking to the image URLs in the data, and link back to
  videotapes.io where you can.
- **Please don't** bulk-download the images or re-host the collection. The image server may limit how fast one
  machine can fetch, to keep it quick for everyone.
- **A bulk archive**, if and when there is one, will be announced here, with how to get it. That will be the way to take
  a full copy of the images.

## What is never shared

Downloads and the API carry no personal data: no accounts, email addresses or internet addresses. They don't say which
website, seller or person a scan came from either (only the general kind of source), and rejected or withdrawn sleeves
are left out.

## Takedowns and corrections

- **Rights holders:** to have artwork removed, write to videotapesdotio@gmail.com or use
  [videotapes.io/contact](https://videotapes.io/contact). Withdrawn sleeves are removed from the site, the API and the
  image server, and left out of later data releases.
- **Corrections:** "Suggest an edit" on any film page, or open an issue here.

## The API

Reading the live API needs a token for now: ask at videotapesdotio@gmail.com. Limits and endpoints are in
[docs/api.md](docs/api.md). If you want everything, use a data release rather than paging through the API.
